A little more Unbound

From a file to a little freedom

Import. Transfer.
Listen offline.

  1. 01

    Bring your own MP3s.

    Open the Files picker on iPhone and choose your non-protected MP3s. Audiobooks, talks, language lessons: if it is your audio, it belongs in your library.

  2. 02

    Send them to your Watch. Pro

    Install Unbound on your paired Apple Watch. With Unbound Pro active, start a transfer from iPhone and let it finish.

  3. 03

    Leave your phone behind.

    Connect your headphones to Apple Watch, open Unbound, and play your transferred audio offline. Your iPhone can stay at home.
