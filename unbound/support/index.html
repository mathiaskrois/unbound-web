Quick help

  • A purchase is missing: Open Unbound → Library Actions → Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Watch transfer is unavailable: Confirm the Watch is paired, install Unbound through the Watch app on iPhone, open it once on Watch, and keep Bluetooth enabled.
  • An MP3 will not import: Confirm the file is a valid, non-protected MP3 accessible through the Files picker.
  • LibriVox is unavailable: Pull to refresh and retry on a working internet connection; LibriVox or Internet Archive may occasionally be unavailable.

When you contact us

Please include your device model, iOS or watchOS version, Unbound version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send private audio files.
